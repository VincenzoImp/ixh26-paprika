⑥ Key lost or stolen? Revoke it (no money moves)

For a key that was stolen or copied: the key signs its own revocation and the device is flagged; no vote, no money. Anyone holding the key can revoke it, including a thief. The owner then registers a new key.

③ Commit

UMA's voters will decide: is this photo a fake, i.e. not a genuine capture by key K? They say FAKE only on public, checkable evidence (UMA's rule UMIP-170). The accuser must prove it.

Reveal

Reveal sends the case to UMA's vote. UMA holds both stakes (B from each side) until the vote, then pays the winner. Paprika disputes its own claim at once, so every accusation is voted on, never won by silence.
